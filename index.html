Q1. What services does Pure HTML offer?

Pure HTML specializes in three core services: web design, frontend development, and developer staffing. Our design and development solutions deliver fast, accessible interfaces for launch, growth, and compliance. Our staffing services provide project-ready professionals to support engineering, product, and design initiatives across the world.

We combine modern technologies like semantic HTML, CSS Grid, Sass, and lightweight JavaScript with experienced, certified designers. From wireframes and visual design to responsive layouts and usability testing, our outputs are standards-compliant, CMS-compatible, and trusted across industries for quality and efficiency.

Pure HTML uses hand-written markup, modular CSS, and progressive enhancement to build interfaces with pixel-level accuracy. We produce pages, components, and style guides that help designers, engineers, and product managers plan, validate, or redesign with confidence. Our builds are responsive, fast-turnaround, and tailored to each project’s scope.

Pure HTML deploys qualified and skill-tested professionals for both short- and long-term projects. Roles include frontend and backend engineers, UI designers, QA testers, accessibility specialists, DevOps engineers, technical writers, and team leads. Each professional is onboarded, insured, and fully managed by Pure HTML to ensure seamless client integration.

Yes. Pure HTML works closely with government agencies and public contractors to deliver design, development, and staffing services for portals, forms, dashboards, and public information sites. We understand the accessibility landscape and provide documentation, staff, and technical outputs that meet public sector standards and planning requirements.

Pure HTML supports a wide range of sectors including e-commerce, education, healthcare, finance, media, nonprofit, SaaS, and public services. Our services are tailored to each industry’s pace, compliance requirements, and technical needs—ensuring clean code, user-readiness, and seamless staff deployment at every stage of a project.

Our frontend outputs include responsive pages (.html, .css, .js), reusable components, style guides, and CMS-ready templates compatible with WordPress, Webflow, and Strapi. Clients also receive annotated documentation and interactive prototypes—ideal for approvals, redesign planning, content handoff, and accessibility compliance.

We maintain a robust pool of pre-vetted, project-ready professionals. Depending on role and time zone, we can deploy engineers, designers, and support staff within 7 to 12 working days. All staffing comes with full HR support, documentation, and onboarding to ensure fast integration into your team and no project delays.

We use a combination of semantic HTML5, modern CSS, Sass, Vite, and lightweight JavaScript. Our tool stack includes Figma, VS Code, Git, Lighthouse, and BrowserStack. These tools enable us to deliver precise, CMS-compatible outputs for responsive layouts, component libraries, and launch-ready documentation.
